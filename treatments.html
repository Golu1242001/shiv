Treatment options

Professional rehabilitation techniques tailored to your needs.

Our physiotherapy care may combine hands-on therapy, electrical stimulation, supportive taping, soft tissue techniques and active rehabilitation depending on the assessment.

👐

Manual Therapy

Hands-on techniques may help improve mobility, tissue movement and comfort in affected joints and muscles.

Potential use cases: stiffness, restricted movement, postural strain, musculoskeletal discomfort.

Book Appointment →
⚡

Electro Therapy

Electrical stimulation may support muscle activation and pain-relief strategies within a broader rehabilitation plan.

Potential use cases: muscle weakness, pain management, support during rehab.

Book Appointment →
🔁

TENS

Transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation is used in some pain-management programs for symptom support.

Potential use cases: discomfort management and support with movement-related pain.

Book Appointment →
🌊

Ultrasound Therapy

Therapeutic ultrasound may be used to support tissue healing and improve movement comfort in selected case plans.

Potential use cases: soft tissue concerns, recovery support, stiffness.

Book Appointment →
🩺

Cupping Therapy

Cupping can be used in some settings as part of a broader rehab plan, particularly where guided relief is considered appropriate.

Potential use cases: muscle tightness, postural strain, recovery support.

Book Appointment →
🩹

Taping

Supportive taping may help with joint or postural support and movement awareness during activity and rehab.

Potential use cases: posture support, joint support, movement control.

Book Appointment →
💉

Dry Needling

Dry needling may be used in carefully selected musculoskeletal conditions to support relief and rehabilitation.

Potential use cases: muscle trigger points, tightness, movement limitations.

Book Appointment →
🧴

Vacuum Therapy

Vacuum-based therapy may support soft tissue release and movement comfort as part of individualized therapy.

Potential use cases: mobility restrictions, fascia-related discomfort, recovery support.

Book Appointment →
📡

ISTM

Instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilization may be used to support soft tissue movement and function in selected cases.

Potential use cases: tissue mobility limitations, tightness, recovery planning.

Book Appointment →
