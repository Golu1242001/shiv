Our services

Physiotherapy services designed for better movement and recovery.

Our treatment plans combine care, clinical assessment and focused rehabilitation for pain relief, mobility, posture and functional progress.

👐

Manual Therapy

Hands-on techniques used to improve joint mobility, muscle comfort, and tissue movement as part of a broader rehabilitation plan.

  • Suitable for stiffness, muscular tightness, and movement limitations

⚡

Electro Therapy

Therapeutic electrical stimulation may be used in selected cases to support muscle activation and pain management strategies.

  • May be suitable when muscle retraining or pain relief is part of the plan
Book Appointment →
🔁

TENS

Transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation is used in some pain management plans to support comfort and mobility.

  • May be suitable for pain management in selected conditions
Book Appointment →
🌊

Ultrasound Therapy

Therapeutic ultrasound may support soft tissue healing and movement function in selected treatment plans.

  • Often considered for soft tissue, inflammatory, or mobility concerns
Book Appointment →
🩺

Cupping Therapy

Traditional cupping may be used where clinically appropriate as part of a broader wellness and rehab plan.

  • Suitable under guided assessment for musculoskeletal discomfort
Book Appointment →
🩹

Taping

Supportive taping may help improve posture, movement awareness, and joint stability during functional tasks.

  • Used in selected cases to support motion control and symptom relief
Book Appointment →
💉

Dry Needling

Needling techniques may be used in carefully selected musculoskeletal cases as part of a comprehensive treatment plan.

  • Considered in cases of trigger points or muscle tightness
Book Appointment →
🧴

Vacuum Therapy

Vacuum-therapy techniques may assist with soft tissue release and mobility support in selected rehabilitation plans.

  • Often used alongside exercise and manual interventions

📡

ISTM

Instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilization may be used to support tissue movement and function, depending on the assessment.

  • May support care for fascial and soft tissue discomfort
