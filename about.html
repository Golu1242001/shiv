About the clinic

Thoughtful physiotherapy care for real recovery goals.

At Shiv Physiotherapy and Wellness Centre, every treatment plan is designed around the individual’s condition, mobility, goals and recovery needs.

Consultant Physiotherapist

Dr. Surbhi Singh

BPT, MPT (ORTHO)

Dr. Surbhi Singh blends clinical assessment, rehabilitation planning, and patient-centered guidance to support mobility, pain management, and physical function.

Her approach focuses on understanding each patient’s movement limitations, functional goals, and recovery needs before creating a personalized treatment strategy.

  • Patient-centered care for adults and children
  • Assessment-based treatment planning
  • Focused on mobility, posture, strength, and function
